Hi Keira,

Happy New Year po! I truly hope this year has started gently for you and that you’ve been doing well. I hope you were able to rest, relax, and really enjoy your vacation po. I’ve been thinking about you a lot, and I just wanted to check in through this letter, not to demand anything, but simply to speak honestly with this letter.

I want to tell you that I haven’t moved on from us. I don’t think I’m trying to either. What we had mattered to me deeply, and it still does. I also want to be honest and say that I miss you so much sobra. I miss the conversations, your presence, and the small moments we used to share, and even now, mahal parin kita ng sobrang-sobra. But at the same time, I understand why we needed to step back. I see now that there were things we both needed to face and grow through on our own.

Over the past weeks, I’ve been reflecting a lot about myself, my actions, my shortcomings, and the ways I can become a better person for myself. I’m learning that growth isn’t about rushing back to what we lost, but about becoming someone healthier, steadier, and more emotionally grounded. That’s something I want to commit to right now, even if it takes time.

I don’t want to pressure you or make you feel like you owe me anything po. This letter isn’t meant to pull you back before you’re ready, or if you ever decide you’re not. I respect your space, your healing, and your journey. I also want to say that I’m genuinely grateful that, even after everything, we’re still able to be friends parin. That means a lot to me and I truly value having you in my life in any form.

I also want to say this, regardless of where life takes us, I hope you keep choosing to be happy. I hope you continue doing the things that bring you peace, growth, and light. You deserve a life that feels calm, fulfilling, and true to who you are, and I hope this year gives you reasons to smile and believe in yourself even more.

I want you to know that I still stand to what I’ve said in the past. The promise I made is to love you no matter what, is something I carry sincerely, without expectations and please know this as well, if there ever comes a time when you need help, support, or simply someone to be there for you, I will always be here. Not to intrude, but to show up, listen, when, and if you need it.

Even in the quiet moments, there are parts of you that stay with me. The way you made things feel lighter, the warmth you brought into my life, and the sense of home I found in you. Loving you taught me patience, depth, and sincerity, and those are things I carry with me every day. No matter how time changes us, the love I have for you remains gentle, honest, and real in some way. I have faith that growth doesn’t separate people who truly matter, it prepares them.

I believe that the work we’re doing on ourselves now could one day allow us to meet again with stronger hearts, clearer intentions, and deeper love than before. I’ll be honest as well nalang din, I still hope and pray that someday our paths may find their way back to each other. Not to return to what we once had, but to build something new on a stronger foundation, slowly and without rushing, where both of us feel safe, understood, and genuinely loved.

Until that time comes, I’m willing to wait patiently, allowing us both our space to grow into the person we’re meant to be. For now, I believe it’s important that we focus on ourselves, on becoming whole and secure as a person and if the right time ever comes, I truly hope it’s still us. I’m not searching for anyone else or trying to replace what we shared. I’ve come to realize that my heart still holds a space for you, and I’m choosing that with patience rather than force.

I also want to thank you for the moments we shared and the lessons you brought into my life. I hope this year gives you peace, clarity, and happiness, whatever form that takes for you.

Take care ka always and I’ll always be here for you,
— Poy
